# Brainstorm: Visual Lab UI Redesign

**Date:** 2026-09-09

## Ideas Explored
- **Option A (Enterprise Lab / Cisco-style):** Clear columns, sharp borders, collapsible panels, neutral colors with single accent. Professional, clean.
- **Option B (Academic Canvas / PhET-style):** Canvas-first approach, maximum space for simulations, minimalist academic palette, drawer-like toolbars.
- **Option C (Modern IDE):** Grid layout with resizable panes, VS Code/Jupyter style. Technical, structured.

## User's Direction
The user chose a combination of **A + B**.
They want the "academic canvas" layout where the central workspace is maximized and dominant, but framed by the "enterprise lab" aesthetics—sharp, structured panels that are visible by default but can be collapsed/toggled to expand the lab space.
Aesthetics must be professional ("sang"), clean, free of chaotic colors, and devoid of childish icons.
The focus is on **Desktop** screens. Both Light and Dark modes are required, but they must be flat, high-contrast, and designed specifically to maintain focus on the academic content, rather than looking overly "AI" or glossy.

## Open Questions
None. The layout structure, aesthetic tone, and device focus are clarified.

## Risks
- Balancing a feature-rich UI with minimalism: Ensuring that we can fit necessary lab controls into the collapsible panels without making them cluttered.
- Dark mode execution: Flat dark mode can sometimes feel heavy if contrast isn't handled correctly; typography needs strict attention.
