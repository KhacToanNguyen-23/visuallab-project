# Brainstorm Report: Visual Lab Cards & Grade Filter Removal

**Date:** 2026-09-10

## Ideas Explored
1. **High-Density Table View**: Text-only table with rows for subject area, title, description, and action buttons. (Dismissed: Hard for students to quickly recognize labs without visual cues).
2. **Visual Card Grid (Bento/SimCard Grid)**: Replaces text tables with an attractive image card grid. Each card displays a high-resolution preview image of the simulation, title, subject badge, description, and an instant launch button. (Selected)

## User's Direction
- Remove grade-level filtering (Grade 10, 11, 12).
- Replace table view on Dashboard with Visual Simulation Cards containing images and clear action buttons so students can instantly see and click into labs.

- None.

## Risks
